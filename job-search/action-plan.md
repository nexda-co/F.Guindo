## Phase 1 — Unlock (Week 1–2)

These must be done before any application can succeed.

- [ ] **Apply for Indiana Pharmacy Technician License** — $25 fee to Indiana Board of Pharmacy. Without this, employers in Indiana cannot legally hire you for pharmacy tech work, even remotely.
- [ ] **Confirm PTCB or ExCPT certification is current and active** — If expired or not yet taken, schedule the exam immediately. PTCB is the gold standard accepted by all employers in this list.
- [ ] **Update portfolio** at https://fatoumataguindo.vercel.app/ — Add PTCB certification number and Indiana license number once received. Employers will look at this link.
- [ ] **Set up a professional LinkedIn profile** — Link to the portfolio. Add pharmacy tech certification, languages spoken (French, Bambara, Tamasheq), and Indiana location.

## Phase 2 — Apply (Week 2–4)

Apply to these companies in priority order.

### Priority 1 — PhilRx / Phil.us
- **Why first:** Remote-first company, entry-level roles available, values patient communication skills
- **Role to apply for:** Patient Support Pharmacy Solutions Coordinator OR Registered Pharmacy Technician (Part-Time)
- **Apply at:** https://phil.us/careers/
- **Tip:** In cover letter, highlight multilingual skills — French speakers are valuable for diverse patient populations

### Priority 2 — Cigna / Accredo (Evernorth)
- **Why second:** Strong training program, 100% remote, equipment provided by company
- **Role to apply for:** PACE Pharmacy Technician WFH or Work-From-Home Pharmacy Tech – Indianapolis
- **Apply at:** https://www.indeed.com/q-the-cigna-group-l-indiana-jobs.html
- **Tip:** Accredo prefers 2 years experience — apply anyway; certification + Indiana license + bilingual = strong profile

### Priority 3 — CVS Caremark
- **Why third:** Large employer, stable, multiple shift options including evening shifts
- **Role to apply for:** Remote Call Center Pharmacy Technician OR Prior Authorization Tech (12pm–8:30pm)
- **Apply at:** https://www.indeed.com/q-cvs-caremark-remote-pharmacy-jobs.html
- **Tip:** Evening shift (12pm–8:30pm EST) is often less competitive — ideal for balancing other commitments

### Priority 4 — Walgreens Remote / Telepharmacy
- **Why:** Has a Pharmacy Technician Trainee pathway — explicitly entry-level
- **Apply at:** https://jobs.walgreens.com/en/search-jobs/remote%20pharmacy%20technician/1242/1

### Priority 5 — Centene Corporation
- **Why:** Multiple remote states, good pay range, coordinator roles are accessible
- **Role to apply for:** Pharmacy Operations Coordinator I (Remote – 15 states)
- **Apply at:** https://jobs.centene.com
